Safety

Kindness is not optional. Safety is shared.

A welcoming community needs clear boundaries. These are simple rules, written so you know what they mean in practice.

  • Treat people like people. Do not insult, degrade or deliberately embarrass someone.
  • No hate speech or discrimination based on sexual orientation, gender identity, race, disability, religion or any other personal characteristic.
  • No harassment. If someone asks you to stop contacting them, stop.
  • Do not publish somebody else's private information, screenshots or personal details without their permission.
  • No threats, sexual harassment, stalking, impersonation or targeted intimidation.
  • Keep disagreements about ideas. Do not turn them into attacks on the person.
  • Do not spam, scam people, flood discussions or use the community for unsolicited advertising.
  • If you see something harmful, report it rather than starting a pile-on. Moderators can deal with it properly.

When something goes wrong

Use the community reporting tools where available. Keep evidence private and avoid reposting harmful material. If there is an immediate threat to someone’s safety, contact an appropriate local emergency or professional service.
