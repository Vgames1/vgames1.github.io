Privacy

Your privacy matters.

This website is deliberately simple. It does not need to know everything about you to point you towards the community.

What this site does

This website is a public information page. It does not ask you to create an account here. When you follow the community link, you leave this site and the destination site's own policies apply.

Analytics and tracking

This version does not require advertising trackers or invasive analytics to work. Hosting providers and third-party services may still process normal technical request data such as IP addresses or browser information as part of delivering a website.

External links

Links to other services are provided for convenience. Check their privacy policies before sharing personal information.
