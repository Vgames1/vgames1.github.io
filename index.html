WHY WE'RE HERE

A place where you don't have to explain yourself first.

Being LGBTQ+ can mean different things to different people. Some of us are out, some are figuring things out, some are looking for friends, and some simply want a place where they can breathe.

This community is here for all of that. Ask the awkward question. Share the good news. Talk about dating, identity, family, Pride, work, relationships or everyday life. You don't need to have the perfect words.

Come curious. Leave people feeling respected.

💬

Talk openly

Share what is on your mind without turning every conversation into a debate.

🫶

Find your people

Meet LGBTQ+ people and genuine allies who understand that everyone's journey is different.

✨

Celebrate the wins

Coming out, finding love, making friends or simply having a good day all count.

OUR GROUND RULES

Simple rules. Real people.

We want this to feel safe without making it feel stiff. The basics are simple: be decent, respect boundaries and leave room for people who are still finding their voice.

01

Be respectful

Speak to people with kindness. No insults, bullying, mocking or deliberately hurtful comments.

02

No hate or discrimination

Slurs, homophobia, biphobia, transphobia, racism, sexism or identity-based attacks are not welcome here.

03

No harassment

Do not repeatedly message, target, threaten or shame someone after they have asked you to stop.

04

Respect privacy

Never share someone's private details, messages, photos, location or story without permission.

05

Keep it consensual

No pressure for sex, dates, photos, personal information or anything else someone has said no to.

06

No scams or spam

Don't use the community to scam people, flood conversations with promotion or push suspicious offers.
