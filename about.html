About us

Community first. Always.

This is a people-first space for LGBTQ+ people and allies. We want it to feel friendly enough for a quick hello and safe enough for a difficult conversation.

What we are about

We are building a community where people can meet without having to perform a version of themselves for anyone else. Some people arrive looking for friends, some have a question, and some simply want to know they are not alone. All of those reasons are valid.

What we value

Kindness matters here. So does honesty, privacy, curiosity and giving people room to be different. We do not expect everyone to agree on everything, but we do expect people to treat one another with basic respect.

A human community

Behind every profile is a real person with a life outside the screen. Before you post, ask yourself whether you would say the same thing to someone sitting next to you. It is a simple rule, but it goes a long way.
