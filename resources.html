Resources

Useful stuff, without the fuss.

Sometimes you need an answer, not a lecture. We are collecting clear, trustworthy resources that people can actually use.

Finding support

If you need professional, legal, medical or crisis support, use an appropriate qualified service rather than relying on a community post alone. If something feels urgent or unsafe, seek local emergency help.

Community knowledge

Members can share experiences and recommendations, but personal experience is not automatically expert advice. Ask questions, check important claims and give people room to say what worked for them without presenting it as universal.

Adding a resource

If you know a genuinely useful organisation, guide or service, send it to us with a short note about what it does and who it helps. We would rather have a small list of useful links than a giant directory nobody trusts.
